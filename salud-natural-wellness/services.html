¶ 04 Before you book — FYI

A little preparation goes far. Un poco de preparación hace mucho.

Please read the following before your appointment. Preparing your body helps you get the most from your session — and all required forms are completed before you arrive. Por favor lee lo siguiente antes de tu cita. Preparar tu cuerpo te ayuda a aprovechar al máximo tu sesión — y todos los formularios requeridos se completan antes de llegar.

  • Stay well-hydrated in the days before — drink plenty of water.
  • Eat a light meal a few hours before. Avoid heavy, greasy, or spicy foods.
  • Avoid eating within two hours of your appointment — if needed, only a small piece of fruit.
  • Refrain from alcohol and caffeine — they dehydrate and reduce effectiveness.
  • Allow yourself time before and after to stay calm and unrushed.
  • Please refrain from heavy perfume, cologne, or scented lotions.
  • Silence your phone or use airplane mode — our office is an EMF-reduced zone.
  • Print and complete all forms before your appointment.
  • Please arrive on time — and allow a few extra minutes if using GPS.
  • Do not arrive more than 15 minutes early; we may still be with another client.
  • Arriving more than 10 minutes late may be treated as a no-show.
  • If there's no answer, please wait in the seating area — we may be in session.
  • Don't eat or drink immediately after — wait until you're home or near a restroom.
  • First meal: avoid beans, nuts, corn, and cabbage — they can cause gas.
  • Avoid greasy, creamy, or alcoholic foods afterward.
  • Hydrate well — water equal to half your body weight in ounces daily.
  • Chew thoroughly and don't drink fluids with meals.
  • Add live foods; reduce flour, pasta, cheese, dairy, and rice.
  • A full aftercare guide is provided at your visit.
  • Abdominal surgery within the past six months — we cannot proceed with colonics.
  • Over 340 pounds — we cannot perform the treatment due to table capacity.
  • When in doubt, message us first and we'll help you find the right option.
  • A 50% deposit is required; balance is due the day of service.
  • We accept PayPal, credit cards, ATH, and cash for the in-person balance.
  • We also accept Health Savings Account (HSA) cards.
  • 48-hour notice is required to cancel or reschedule.
  • All packages are final and must be used within six months.
  • Management may apply your deposit toward other services at its discretion.
